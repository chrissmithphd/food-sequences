# CLASSIFICATION RULES

1. PRODUCT IDENTITY over flavor: "Garlic Crackers" = crackers. "Raspberry Coffee Creamer" = cream_cheese or half_and_half. "Vanilla Yogurt" = yogurt. The main noun is the product; adjectives are flavors.
2. PREPARED DISHES stay as dishes: "Frozen Lasagna" = frozen_lasagna, NOT pasta. "Mac and Cheese" = mac_and_cheese, NOT pasta. "Chicken Soup" = soup, NOT chicken.
3. BRAND names are irrelevant: "Barilla Penne" = pasta. "Kraft Singles" = american_cheese. "Lean Cuisine Chicken Alfredo" = frozen_meal.
4. ORGANIC/FAT LEVEL are attributes: "Organic Banana" = banana. "2% Milk" = milk. "Low Fat Yogurt" = yogurt.
5. NON-FOOD products: Pet food, household cleaners, personal care items — classify into the specific non-food category.
6. If a product truly matches nothing, use the closest reasonable match.
